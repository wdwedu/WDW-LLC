About Perennial

A garden can be beautiful and productive at the same time.

Perennial Home & Gardens combines practical growing help, planning tools, education, digital products and local garden support. Food, flowers, herbs, habitat and outdoor living all belong in the same conversation.

Digital resources are designed for gardeners across U.S. growing zones; local services focus on the Washington metropolitan area and Southern Maryland.
